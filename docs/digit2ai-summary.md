# Digit2AI - High-Level Summary

Source: https://digit2ai.com (and linked pages), researched September 2026.

## What it is

Digit2AI (legal entity Digit2ai LLC) is a company that builds AI-powered,
industry-specific ("vertical") software platforms on top of a shared
technology infrastructure. Rather than one general product, it runs a
portfolio of standalone platforms, each targeting a different industry, so
new verticals can be launched largely pre-built on common AI, CRM, and
automation building blocks.

## Business model

- Builds vertical platforms on shared infrastructure, so new products start
  a large percentage pre-built, letting the company launch new verticals
  faster than competitors building from scratch.
- Each vertical is run as its own product line/brand, together forming a
  diversified portfolio, while creating deep switching costs (stickiness)
  within each market it enters.
- Positions itself as aiming to become a definitive AI platform company for
  small businesses.

## Product portfolio

As of the research date, Digit2AI operates roughly a dozen live platforms
across different industries, including:

- **RinglyPro** - AI call answering, appointment booking, and CRM for
  professional services businesses (an all-in-one AI system that answers
  calls, qualifies leads, books appointments, sends follow-ups, and
  generates marketing content/email campaigns). This repository
  (CRM-Co-Pilot) supports that line of work.
- **TunjoRacing** - motorsports media.
- **Lactotec** - dairy and agritech / dairy plant management.
- **KanchoAI** and **Ronin Brotherhood** - martial arts AI and community.
- **LawnCopilot** - lawn care.
- **Recovery + Nutrition** - wellness and recovery.
- **OrderGoPro** - restaurant ordering.
- **Doctor Picante** - food and beverage wholesale.
- **PixlyPro** - creative services.
- **Enruta** - automotive tech.
- **AIA Store** - AI-based retail monitoring.
- **AIHotelTalent** - hospitality recruitment.

The company describes all of these platforms as live, deployed, and serving
real businesses in production, with several new verticals launched within a
single recent quarter.
